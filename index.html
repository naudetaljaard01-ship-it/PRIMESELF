OFFLINE SELF-CARE GUIDE

WORKS WITHOUT AI

These are general examples, not a diagnosis or a claim that a product is right for every skin type. Check the label and stop using anything that irritates your skin. Ask a pharmacist or trusted adult if you're unsure.

🫧 Gentle skincare picks

  • Cleanser: CeraVe Hydrating Cleanser (often suited to normal-to-dry skin) or Foaming Cleanser (often suited to normal-to-oily skin); Simple Kind to Skin cleanser is another option.
  • Moisturiser: CeraVe Moisturising Lotion, Cetaphil Moisturising Lotion, or Simple Hydrating Light Moisturiser.
  • Daytime sunscreen: broad-spectrum SPF 30–50, for example Eucerin Sun or La Roche-Posay Anthelios. Pick a comfortable option you can use consistently.
  • Budget option: compare Simple and store-brand gentle cleansers/moisturisers; prices and stock vary.

💈 Haircare picks

  • Shampoo: choose by scalp needs; examples include Dove, Sunsilk, or a gentle fragrance-free option if your scalp is sensitive.
  • Conditioner: a basic conditioner from the same range can help detangle and soften hair, especially on the lengths and ends.
  • Where to compare in South Africa: Clicks, Dis-Chem, Checkers, Pick n Pay, and official brand/store websites. Check current prices, ingredients, and stock before buying.

☀️ Simple offline routine

  1. Morning: gentle cleanse (or rinse if your skin feels dry), moisturiser, then sunscreen.
  2. Evening: gentle cleanse, then moisturiser.
  3. Hair: wash when your scalp needs it; avoid adding lots of new products at once.
Important: expensive products aren't automatically better. Patch-test new products, don't use ice directly on your face, and get medical advice for painful, persistent, or worsening skin problems. This guide is stored inside this HTML file and can be read offline.
